# Keep agent review suggestions draft until a person publishes them

An Exploratory Review Run may inspect and test the complete project, but it returns structured Review Suggestions and has no authority to mutate CodeCommit. A publishable suggestion requires concrete, reproducible Review Evidence and displays its Confidence separately from impact; suspected defects that cannot yet be validated remain non-publishable Review Notes. A suggestion may include an exact Suggested Replacement, but that replacement remains inert and may only be published as CodeCommit review-comment content; it never changes the branch. An agent may directly create a new version of a draft suggestion when asked, but prior versions remain visible; a changed technical claim requires new validation rather than inheriting old evidence. The Local Operator owns local suggestion state and may publish only through the connected CodeCommit identity. Publishing opens a preview that shows that AWS identity, exact reviewed revision and anchor, and editable final content before the operator explicitly confirms `Post comment`. The posted comment is a snapshot rather than a live mirror: later draft edits require a separate preview and explicit `Update posted comment`, with both local revision history and remote comment identity preserved. Relevant defects that the pull request did not introduce are displayed separately as non-publishable Review Notes. On publication, a line suggestion is posted at its exact diff position, a file suggestion is posted at the file's first changed line or line 1 as a fallback, and a whole-change suggestion becomes a general pull-request comment. A suggestion grouped by root cause posts once at its primary anchor and lists Related Locations in the body by default; the publication preview may explicitly split it into separate comments. When the pull-request head changes, suggestions from older revisions become stale and cannot be published until a re-review evaluates them against the new immutable head; the re-review may preserve, supersede, or resolve them without erasing their history. Agent-made resolutions require no human confirmation, but must record their rationale, evidence, and exact reviewed revision.
