Work and Connect link both ways. A Work goal's "Open <agent> in Connect" link now opens that agent's stage in Connect, its terminal one tap away; a Connect link with `open=stage` opens the stage, and links without it still open the terminal. An agent's stage links back to its Work goal when the board has exactly one.
