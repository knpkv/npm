Connect's characters live across polls. A creature turns to you once when its agent starts needing you, not on every poll and not on load. Rows keep their creature when another agent joins ahead of them, so its breath and blink carry on. An agent's stage lists the agents it started as named buttons that open their own stages, and its field drifts behind the character with a little parallax, only when the reader allows motion.
