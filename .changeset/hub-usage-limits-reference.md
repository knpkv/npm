Usage Limits now reads like its design: one sentence on whether the hub has room for more work, notices for old or unreadable limits, and one card per tool with its level, state (near limit, limit reached, stale, sign-in required), limit track, resets and a note. A limit is "Sign-in required" only when the account needs a sign-in; any other unreadable limit reads Unknown with its cause, and a tool with no reading still gets an Unknown card. Resets on another day name the day. Loading and a failed first read keep their own simple states. The Limits page drops the host, range and time-zone controls; they stay on Tokens and By tool.
