Connect gains a cast and a stage. A swipeable strip of every agent's character sits above the directory, the ones that need you first. A row or cast member opens the agent's stage: the character large on a field in its own hues, its work as what it is saying, its state and host, and Open terminal. Escape or Close returns focus to whatever opened it. The terminal now opens from the stage rather than straight from the row.
