Usage Limits shows the hub's own tokens as its design draws them: one bar per period with its value printed, and a legend with each tool's total in text. A tool whose limits can't be read is left out of every total and named as excluded, and the title says which tools are counted. Reset times say "next" only a full week out and give the date for a reset already past.
