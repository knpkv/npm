The hub writes its lines as phrases instead of joining facts with middots: "Showing 24 of 30 matching, 30 jobs in all", "Load earlier (6 remaining)", "claude, arch" for an agent, "knpkv/npm#433: existing owner" for a Work job, and "Host activity on SER8" as the page title. The keyboard hint reads "In the list, J and K move, Enter opens details and Esc clears the search", and J/K never act while a field inside the list has focus or a modifier is held. At phone width the activity filters wrap instead of scrolling sideways, so the last one ("Agent") no longer sits past the screen edge. The middot ast-grep rule now covers herdr-approvals.
